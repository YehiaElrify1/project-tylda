3 CREATIVE IDEA (The A + V + O Concept)
Concept

Centralized smart healthcare platform uniting Patients, Doctors, Pharmacies & Labs with an Admin governance layer.

Inspiration

Overcoming Egypt's fragmented paper records, unverified providers, pharmacy dispatch delays, and diagnostic access barriers.

Unique Value (A + V + O)

Automate e-Rx & booking, Visualize AI test OCR & analytics, Optimize care with Firestore & Hive.

4 PROBLEM & AUDIENCE (5 Stakeholders)
Challenge

Fragmented health records, unverified practitioner accounts, delayed emergency access & manual pharmacy fulfillment.

Target Users & Roles

Patients (triage, bookings), Doctors (EHR, e-Rx), Pharmacies (orders), Labs (reports) & Admin (governance).
